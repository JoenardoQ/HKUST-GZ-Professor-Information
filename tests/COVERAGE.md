Applicability notes: malformed inputs, duplicates, conflicting dates, retry exhaustion, source unavailability, translation ordering, and trust-boundary URLs are covered at the deterministic unit boundary. Live latency, CAPTCHA behavior, local xRxiv dump size, upstream pagination/version skew, service-specific rate limits, and semantic translation quality remain integration risks. Retrieval workers may run independent source/profile shards, but each checkpoint has one writer and the final deterministic assembler is sequential. The suite covers the declared P0 deterministic behaviors with synthetic fixtures; it does not establish exhaustive publication retrieval, upstream availability, author correctness outside the OpenAlex gate, or translation quality.
